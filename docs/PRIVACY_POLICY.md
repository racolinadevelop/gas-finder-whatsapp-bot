# Privacy Policy

**Gas Finder Bot**

**Effective date:** September 14, 2026

Gas Finder Bot is a software project that helps users find nearby gas stations and compare available fuel information through WhatsApp.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application processes, why it is used, and how it is handled.

## Information We Process

When you interact with Gas Finder Bot through WhatsApp, the application may process:

- Your WhatsApp sender identifier or phone number
- Messages you send to the bot
- Location coordinates that you voluntarily share through WhatsApp
- Technical information required to process webhook events
- Gas station search preferences, such as fuel type or sorting option

Gas Finder Bot only processes location information when you choose to share a location with the bot.

## How We Use Your Information

The information is used only to provide the functionality of the application.

For example, location coordinates are used to:

1. Search for gas stations near the location you provided.
2. Retrieve available gas station and fuel information.
3. Calculate approximate distances.
4. Determine nearby, cheaper, or estimated best options.
5. Send the results back to you through WhatsApp.

Your location is not used for advertising or profiling.

## Location Information

Gas Finder Bot requires a location to perform nearby gas station searches.

Location information is provided voluntarily by the user through WhatsApp.

The current development version of Gas Finder Bot does not intentionally store location information in a permanent application database.

During development and troubleshooting, webhook information may temporarily appear in local application logs.

## Message Information

Messages sent to the bot may be processed to determine what action the user wants the bot to perform.

For example, future versions may allow commands such as:

`regular`

`premium`

`diesel`

`closest`

`cheapest`

`best`

The current development version does not intentionally store conversation history in a permanent application database.

## Third-Party Services

Gas Finder Bot uses third-party services to provide its functionality.

### Meta WhatsApp Business Platform

WhatsApp messages are sent and received using the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform.

Meta may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

### Google Places API

Location coordinates may be sent to Google Places API to search for nearby gas stations and retrieve available place and fuel information.

Google processes these requests according to its own privacy policies and service terms.

## Data Sharing

Gas Finder Bot does not sell personal information.

Information is shared with third-party services only when necessary to provide the application's functionality, such as:

- Meta for WhatsApp messaging
- Google for nearby gas station searches

## Data Storage and Retention

The current development version of Gas Finder Bot does not intentionally maintain a permanent database containing user locations or WhatsApp conversations.

Some information may temporarily exist in:

- Application memory
- Development logs
- Infrastructure logs
- Third-party service systems

Future versions that introduce persistent storage, user accounts, search history, favorites, alerts, or similar features will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

## Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect application credentials and user information.

Sensitive credentials such as API keys and access tokens are stored using environment variables and are not intentionally committed to the public source code repository.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

## User Choices

Users control whether they share their location with Gas Finder Bot.

If a user does not want the application to process location information, they should not send their location to the bot.

Users may stop interacting with the bot at any time.

## Data Deletion Requests

If persistent user data is introduced in a future version, users will be able to request deletion of applicable stored information.

## Children's Privacy

Gas Finder Bot is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

If it becomes known that information from a child has been stored unintentionally, reasonable steps will be taken to remove it.

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Gas Finder Bot evolves.

Changes may be necessary when new features are introduced, including databases, user accounts, search history, alerts, or production deployment.
